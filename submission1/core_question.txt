Business question (Meridian Bank retention):
"Which at-risk customers should relationship managers prioritize for outreach — the ones
 with the highest revenue at risk — and what retention action, offer, and predicted net
 value does the model recommend for each?"

Answered by joining two synced UC gold tables served from Lakebase (both read-only, low-latency):
  retention.gold_open_atrisk  (at-risk positions)  ⨝  retention.gold_nba_recommendations (next-best-action)
on customer_id. See core_query.sql / core_query_result.json.
